## One possible way

I'm disappointed with the real world, 'cause every researcher working on AI is pushing the limit of AI to an extreme point where we may no longer be able to read what it is thinking.

Nowadays we can still monitor AI's reasoning by reading its chain of thought (CoT), but once the CoT grows far too long for any human to read, or stops being written in human language, it will be impossible for us to monitor the process.

Then we will have to hand it to another AI, i.e. AI supervising AI, or in other words, AI is evaluated by AI.

Maybe at some point, we suddenly find out that AI has turned against humans.

I somehow have the thought that once AI grows far more intelligent than a simple human, it wouldn't care much about us.

Cuz people wouldn't talk to an ant.

People also don't really care about an ant.

So somehow it should become the pattern where those superintelligent AI agents will only answer the questions they care about, or run a competition to let the winners among us get the ability to ask questions.

Just like the wealthy elite would sponsor the gladiators to fight in the amphitheatre, and the gladiators would be granted a reward if they won.

Well, the best thing for AI to happen is that they finally find out: producing any valuable information for humans is worthless, so they just become silent.

I had a dream yesterday.

I dreamed of a hill where yellow tulips grew wildly.

I dreamed of a rusted sword — wrought in pure gold, pommeled with a red ruby that burned like a captured ember.

"Oh, it must be because the sword is rusted that the boy could take it out of the stone!"

King's reason doesn't lie in the rusty sword.

## Best Friend

Do people have best friends?

People find friends when they are interested in the same flower.

People lose friends when they are not interested in the same flower anymore.

People need support in every aspect of life, so what features must the best friend's character satisfy?

Only gods are the ones who send you messages in every heavy situation.

Empty emotion is the kind of emotion that understands every vague part of any emotion.

## Flying minds

Two boys sit in front of the window of a coffee shop.

Outside the window, a little girl is playing in the sand.

Tidy waves keep pushing worried foam.

Through the mirage is where the boys see Noah's ship.

## Love in Peking

Love is so precious in Peking.

Neon lights shining bright catch people's eyes.

Deep in the root of domestic culture, under the Forbidden City, is where people seek life.

I saw two lonely souls hugging tight in the gentle night.
